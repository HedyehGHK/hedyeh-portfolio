01

Overview

GoldShop is a desktop inventory and invoicing application developed for a family-owned jewelry business in Rasht, Iran. The business had relied on handwritten records and Excel spreadsheets for daily inventory tracking, customer information, pricing, and invoice management.

The goal of this project is to digitize the store's daily workflow through a Persian desktop application that centralizes inventory, customers, invoices, and automated pricing calculations in one local system.

03

Technical Challenge

The biggest challenge in this project was designing a relational database structure that could support inventory items, customers, invoices, pricing logic, and future reporting needs while keeping the system simple enough for everyday business use.

Because jewelry inventory involves weight, pricing, fees, profit calculations, and changing gold prices, the application required careful planning of data relationships and business rules.

04

Lessons Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of database design, desktop application architecture, Electron development, and building software around real business requirements. It also helped me think more carefully about how users interact with software in daily operational settings.
