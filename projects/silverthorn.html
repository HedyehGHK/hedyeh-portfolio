Overview

During my co-op placement with the Toronto District School Board, I worked on the Silverthorn Collegiate Institute website using Evoq CMS, built on DNN. The work focused on improving page structure, updating content, and creating a cleaner browsing experience for students, families, and staff.

This project gave me hands-on experience working with a real organizational website, following accessibility expectations, and making practical updates within an existing content management system.
